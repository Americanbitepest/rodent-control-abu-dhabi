Rodent Control in Abu Dhabi

Rats and mice don't just steal food — they chew wires, contaminate stores and close businesses.

Rodents thrive wherever there's food, water and shelter — which describes most of urban Abu Dhabi. Roof rats run along boundary walls and enter through roof gaps; house mice squeeze through holes as small as a coin. Restaurants, warehouses and villas with gardens see them most. American Bite Pest Control combines trapping, baiting and proofing so they don't come back.

Signs of rodents

Droppings near food stores and along walls, gnaw marks on packaging and cables, scratching sounds in ceilings at night, greasy rub marks along skirting, and nests of shredded material in quiet corners. A single sighting usually means more nearby — rodents are social and breed fast.

How professional rodent control works

Effective control has three parts. First, inspection to find entry points, runs and nesting sites. Second, removal — traps and secured bait stations placed along their routes, checked and serviced on schedule. Third, and most important, proofing: sealing every gap larger than a coin with rodent-proof materials, fitting door sweeps and screening vents. Killing without proofing is a temporary fix; new rodents just move into the same holes. https://bitepestcontrol.com

Restaurants and food businesses

A rodent sighting can fail a municipality inspection and damage a restaurant's reputation overnight. Food premises need locked, tamper-resistant bait stations on a serviced schedule, documented visits, and proofing of bin stores and delivery areas. Kitchens should be treated as part of a programme, not a one-off emergency.

Villas, warehouses and offices

Villa gardens, boundary walls and sheds give rodents cover; warehouses give them food and miles of harbourage. Offices see them in false ceilings and server rooms where cables get chewed. A monitoring programme — stations checked monthly, entry points kept sealed — keeps properties rodent-free year-round.
